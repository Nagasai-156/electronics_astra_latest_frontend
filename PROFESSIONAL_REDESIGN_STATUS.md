## Design System Used

### Colors
- **Primary**: #003845 (teal/dark cyan)
- **Secondary**: #00999e (cyan)
- **Accent**: #f5c542 (yellow/gold)
- **Neutral**: #e6f4f1 (light teal)

### Gradients
- from-primary-50 to-primary-100
- from-secondary-50 to-secondary-100
- from-accent-50 to-accent-100

### Borders
- border-3 or border-2 with border-black
- Rounded corners: rounded-xl, rounded-2xl

### Shadows
- shadow-[4px_4px_0px_0px_rgba(0,0,0,1)]
- shadow-[3px_3px_0px_0px_rgba(0,0,0,1)]

### Typography
- Headers: font-black text-xl
- Body: font-semibold text-gray-700/800
- Code: font-mono font-bold
